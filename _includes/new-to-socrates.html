New to SoCraTes?

Everyone is welcome here, whether you are in your first job or have twenty years behind you. You don't need to be a speaker or a senior; you just need to be curious and happy to share the weekend with like-minded people.

Nothing is scheduled in advance. On the first evening everyone proposes sessions together, and you join whatever interests you. Present a topic, pair on some code, sit back and listen, or simply chat over a coffee: all are equally welcome.

It's a warm and inclusive gathering, and people go out of their way to make newcomers feel at home. If you'd like to know what we expect of one another, have a read of our Code of Conduct.

Not sure whether it's for you? Come and say hi in the community Slack — we'd love to hear from you.
