COVID-19 still causes illness and long-term harm across the world ([source](https://www.who.int/news/item/03-12-2025-WHO-launches-new-unified-plan-for-countries-to-manage-coronaviruses-COVID-19-and-beyond)). 

We want to keep attendees safe, while at the same time allowing for as much familiarity and community spirit as possible. <br>
Each measure on its own has flaws but together they offer better protection.

For this reason our policy at the conference this year will be:

* **Masks** will be provided and we **strongly recommend** wearing them in all indoor conference spaces (except while eating/drinking)
* Lateral flow **tests** will be provided for each day and are **mandatory** to perform (conference organisers should be notified if the test is positive)
* Air filtering will be provided in meeting rooms that don't have windows to the outside

We encourage you to test if possible before you start travelling. We had one person arrive with covid in 2025 and we are currently preparing a description of what we did and what we can expect from each other when that happens.
