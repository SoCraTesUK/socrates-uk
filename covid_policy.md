## SoCraTes UK COVID Policy

Although the COVID-19 pandemic may feel like a distant memory to many of us, the disease
[still causes illness and long-term harm across the world](https://www.who.int/news/item/03-12-2025-WHO-launches-new-unified-plan-for-countries-to-manage-coronaviruses-COVID-19-and-beyond).
This is particularly impactful for those of us who are vulnerable, or who need to come into contact with loved ones and
others who are vulnerable.

### Masks - provided and strongly recommended

- **Masks are provided**, and we **strongly recommend** wearing a mask when indoors (except while eating/drinking).

### Testing - Provided and mandatory whilst at the conference

- **Lateral flow tests** will be provided for each day and are **mandatory** to perform (conference organisers should be
notified if the test is positive).
- In additional, it is **strongly recommended** that you take a test **before travelling to the conference** if possible.

(One person arrived at SoCraTes UK 2025 with COVID, and it was testing that enabled us to discover this early and to 
prevent other participants from catching it.)

### Air fitering

Air filtering will be provided in meeting rooms that don't have windows to the outside.

### What to do if you test positive for COVID

- You must avoid indoor sessions; the organisers will do our best to organise a video call to dial you in to any
sessions that you want to attend.
- You must avoid the restaurant for breakfast/lunch/dinner (we’ll arrange for hotel staff to deliver meals to your
room).
- Outdoor sessions: feel free to organise and attend outdoor sessions; wear a mask when attending and keep a safe
distance from others whilst attending them.
